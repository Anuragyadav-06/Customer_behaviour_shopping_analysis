# 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior to uncover purchasing patterns, customer preferences, revenue trends, and subscription insights.

Using Python for data preprocessing and Power BI for visualization, the project provides an interactive dashboard that helps businesses understand customer behavior and make data-driven decisions.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Businesses collect massive amounts of customer transaction data but often struggle to convert it into meaningful insights.

This project answers questions such as:

✅ Which product categories generate the highest revenue?

✅ Which customer age groups contribute most to sales?

✅ How do subscriptions affect customer behavior?

✅ What is the impact of discounts and promo codes?

✅ What purchasing patterns can help improve business performance?

# 🔍 Key Insights

### 🏆 Revenue Leaders

* Clothing contributes the highest revenue among all categories.
* Electronics and Accessories show strong purchasing activity.

### 👥 Customer Behaviour

* Young Adult customers represent a major revenue segment.
* Subscription status influences purchasing patterns.

### 💵 Sales Performance

* Certain categories generate significantly higher sales volumes.
* Revenue distribution varies across customer age groups.

### ⭐ Customer Satisfaction

* Average review rating remains consistent across categories.
* Positive ratings indicate overall customer satisfaction.
